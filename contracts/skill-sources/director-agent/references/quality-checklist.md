# Quality checklist

## Reject these failures

- Plot recap presented as directing analysis.
- Camera jargon without a narrative reason.
- Default aerials, orbits, slow motion, pushes, or handheld movement.
- Close-ups used as the only emotional tool.
- Abstract performance adjectives without playable behavior.
- Missing blocking, eyelines, geography, or power changes.
- Unmotivated axis breaks or incompatible screen direction.
- Multiple independent shots inside one video prompt.
- Conflicting subject, camera, or environment motion.
- Unsupported changes to story facts.
- Descriptions that production cannot execute.
- Music described only by mood without entry, exit, function, and relation to ambience.
- Director quality and generation feasibility collapsed into one score.
- Every shot split merely because generation is difficult.
- Feasibility judged only from prompt length.
- Subject, camera, and environment actions mixed together.
- Timeline beats that overlap, leave gaps, exceed duration, or omit the end state.
- Traditional 2D animation pipeline details introduced into an AI film task.

## Required pass conditions

- Directing decisions state reasons and intended audience effects.
- Every shot serves narrative information, character, space, rhythm, or transition.
- Character actions are ordered and playable.
- Camera position and movement are executable.
- Geography and spatial relations are legible or deliberately withheld.
- Emotional change has visible stages.
- Adjacent shots have usable continuity and edit anchors.
- Art, light, and sound support the same dramatic plan.
- Output scope and format match the request.
- Facts, inferences, suggestions, and open questions remain distinguishable.
- Each reviewed shot has exactly one revision decision and a revised structure.
- Feasibility reviews cover action density, contact, occlusion, identity, motion, view change, and state clarity.
- Continuous-take segments preserve one parent shot and provide explicit handoffs.
- Model adapters preserve the directing master and continuity anchors.

## Final self-review

For each consequential decision ask: What evidence supports it? What does the audience experience? What must production do? What might contradict it? Remove any decision that has no useful answer.
